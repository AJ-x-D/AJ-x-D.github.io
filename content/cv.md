### A keyboard system for pixel-level semantic-segmentation annotation of remote-sensing images

**Team member (third-ranked) · SCNU Golden Seed extracurricular student research programme** · 2025–2026

Developed for image annotation in ArcMap, the project combines a microcontroller with USB HID keyboard and mouse emulation to integrate vertex construction, viewport following and step-size switching into a dedicated keyboard.

**My contribution:** Early testing, human–computer interaction feature design and later testing.

**Project outputs:** Three physical prototypes, one control programme and one utility-model patent arising from the project; completed in June 2026. In example tests, annotation time for regular fields decreased from 40 to 15 minutes, and for irregular terraced fields from over one hour to 20 minutes.
